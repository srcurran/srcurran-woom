The iPhone Duo is about to break a lot of apps that use custom navigation components.

The Duo moves tabs and toolbars to the side. Apps built with native UI get that for free, but apps that don't are going to be stuck as is.

This is going to be a big change. For years it was easy to roll your own navigation and not feel out of place. Now, on the Duo, those apps will feel like the ones that clung to skeuomorphism after iOS 7 went flat.

## Capacitor apps are the most exposed

This is an especially big risk for apps built with Capacitor. Capacitor provides no first-party native support for things like tabs or toolbars.

The most obvious path for Capacitor apps in this moment is to redesign and rewrite the entire navigational UI using a third-party plugin. Even then, a plugin that looks native may not actually provide native functionality.

And open apps might be even weirder.

I'm looking forward to playing with this when the new Xcode simulator is released.
